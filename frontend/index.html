A unified defensive dashboard that ingests synthetic threat-intelligence data, normalizes and enriches it, scores organizational risk, visualizes threats, and teaches cybersecurity awareness — combining human defense with technical threat intelligence.

100% offline synthetic data only defensive security SOC-style workflow

⚠️
IOC match ≠ automatic confirmed compromise. An indicator match is evidence for investigation, not proof of a breach. Indicators can be stale, shared by benign infrastructure, or lack context. This dashboard supports analyst judgement — it never replaces it. All data on this site is SYNTHETIC / DEMO ONLY.

Five concepts this dashboard keeps strictly separate

OBSERVATION
A single raw sighting of an artifact in telemetry (e.g. one DNS query seen in logs).
INDICATOR
A validated, normalized artifact (IP, domain, URL, hash, CVE) that can be tracked and enriched.
ALERT
A notification generated when thresholds or correlation rules fire — a cue for triage, not a verdict.
THREAT
An assessed record describing potentially malicious activity, scored for risk and confidence.
INCIDENT
A confirmed event violating security policy — established only by human investigation.

📈 Risk score vs Confidence score

Two different questions — never blended.

Risk score
“How concerning may this indicator/event be?”
Severity 30% · Confidence 25% · Recency 15% · Frequency 10% · Source reliability 10% · Context 10%
Confidence score
“How strong is the evidence behind the intelligence?”
Source reliability, corroboration, observation count, staleness.
💡
Risk 90 / Confidence 25 → potentially serious, but the evidence is weak. · Risk 70 / Confidence 95 → high-confidence intelligence with meaningful risk.
High risk does NOT automatically mean confirmed compromise.
