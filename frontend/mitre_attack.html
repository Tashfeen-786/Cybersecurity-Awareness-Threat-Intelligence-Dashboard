🎯 MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

Adversary-behaviour context for the synthetic threat records — mapped only where behavioural context justifies it. Technique IDs are real ATT&CK IDs; they are never invented when evidence is insufficient.

Tactic

WHY an adversary performs an action — the objective.

Example: Initial Access = getting in.

Technique

HOW the objective may be achieved.

Example: T1566 Phishing — one way to gain initial access.

Sub-technique

A more specific implementation.

Example: T1566.002 Spearphishing Link.

🎯
IOC vs ATT&CK: an IOC tells us WHAT artifact was observed (domain, IP, hash…). ATT&CK helps describe HOW behaviour may relate to adversary techniques. A domain alone proves nothing about technique — mapping requires behavioural context, and this project omits mapping rather than guessing.
